Delete Your LexNote Data

Last updated: April 10, 2025

You can request deletion of your LexNote account and all associated data at any time. We offer two ways to do this.

Option 1: Delete from the app

  1. Open the LexNote app
  2. Go to Settings
  3. Tap your account (signed-in users)
  4. Tap Delete Account
  5. Confirm deletion

Your account and all server-side data will be permanently deleted immediately.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

Send an email to lexnote.app@gmail.com with the subject line "Delete my account" and include the email address associated with your LexNote account. We will process your request within 30 days.

What data is deleted

  • Deleted immediately: Your account (email, password hash), all synced vocabulary data (words, translations, groups, dictionaries)
  • Deleted within 30 days: Backup copies and logs
  • Not affected: Local data stored on your device — this remains until you uninstall the app
